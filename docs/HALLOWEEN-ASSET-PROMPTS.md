# Generated Halloween assets

Created with the built-in image-generation tool, using the existing BOM Halloween
pirate crest as the reference: antique gold, black shield, pirate skull with
tricorn/red bandana, crossed sabres, anchor, orange glow and purple spectral accents.
PNG optimization/resizing preserves generated artwork and alpha where requested.

## Bot and guild set

Located at apps/dashboard/public/event-guide/branding/ and copied to
HALLOWEEN-ASSETS/branding/ in the update archive:

- evil-misfit-avatar.png: 512x512, menacing pirate skull captain, orange eyes,
  purple smoke, circular-crop-safe composition, dark vignette, no lettering.
- evil-misfit-banner.png: 1536x512, haunted ghost fleet/captain setting, orange
  lanterns, purple fog, BOM crest on right, text exactly EVIL MISFIT.
- evil-misfit-role-icon.png: 128x128 alpha, simplified skull/tricorn/red bandana,
  orange eyes, gold rim and purple edge light, no lettering or scenery.
- halloween-server-background.png: 1920x1080, haunted moonlit pirate port,
  ghostly purple fog, pumpkins/lanterns framing a dark center for invitation UI.
- halloween-server-banner.png: 960x540, moonlit ghost galleon and pumpkin-lit
  sea, BOM crest and BAND OF MISFITS, calm upper region for Discord's server title.
- halloween-guild-logo.png: 512x512 alpha, BOM shield, skull as O between B/M,
  crossed sabres/anchor, pumpkins, orange eyes and purple edge glow.

## Three compact trophy emojis

Located at apps/dashboard/public/event-guide/trophies/rank-1.png through rank-3.png
and HALLOWEEN-ASSETS/trophies/:

Prompt set: one square transparent gold/silver/bronze Halloween trophy cup,
centered compact silhouette, thick metal rim, small pirate skull/tricorn/red
bandana and orange eyes, purple accents and tiny pumpkin at base. Simple bold
shapes readable at 32px, clear margin, no lettering, scenery or dangling ornament.
128x128 files are intended for Discord emoji upload. The website has 256x256
versions at event-guide/donation-trophy-1.png through donation-trophy-3.png.
